    How to read it. Hover any column heading for what it means. Every number comes from checks that actually ran — a country is never marked “no slots” for hours nobody looked at (those show hatched on the grid). Next appointment is bookable now; typical wait is the median days from a check to the appointment it offered; soonest seen is the best case in the window; last slot seen is when we last detected one. Dates are the earliest offered on a check — VFS quotes a later date for two or three applicants, and the soonest of them is what counts here.
